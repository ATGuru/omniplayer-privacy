OmniPlayer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Who we are

OmniPlayer is a free music player for Android. Guru Morgan made it. AllTechGuru is the company behind it. The app has no account, no ads, and no paywall. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device. There is a longer introduction on the About page.

2. Information stored on your device

OmniPlayer keeps your library on the phone. We do not run a server that receives your songs, playlists, or listening history.

On the device, the app stores:

  • Songs it finds when you scan the device, and songs you choose to save from Free Music.
  • Titles, artists, albums, genres, track numbers, and lyrics you type or edit.
  • Favorites, play counts, playlists, albums you create, and the last playback position.
  • The license name of a song saved from Free Music, so the player can show that credit.

3. Music already on your phone

Scan Device asks Android for permission to read music on the phone. That permission is used to list songs you already have. Those files stay where they are. If you remove one of those songs from the OmniPlayer library, the file remains on the device and a later scan does not add it back.

4. Free Music

Free Music searches the Internet Archive netlabel collection for recordings that carry a Creative Commons or public-domain license. When you search, filter, open a release, preview a song, or download one, the app contacts archive.org from your device.

That request includes the search words and filters you chose, and the usual connection information a website receives, such as an IP address. OmniPlayer does not attach your name, email, or an account. Internet Archive's own privacy policy applies to that request: archive.org/about/terms.php.

Preview plays a song over the internet and does not save a file. A file is saved only when you tap download. Saved files stay in the app's private storage on the phone.

5. Create New Song

Create New Song opens lyricsintosong.com in your browser. OmniPlayer does not receive what you type on that site, and it does not take a payment there. That site has its own privacy policy: lyricsintosong.com/privacy.

6. What we do not collect

OmniPlayer does not ask you to create an account. It does not collect your email, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising ID. It does not include ads, analytics, or a crash-reporting service. We do not sell personal information.

7. Notifications

The playback notification lets a song keep playing when the screen is off, and lets you pause or skip from the notification or a headset. You can turn notifications off in Android settings.

8. Children

OmniPlayer is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Deleting information

You can delete a song from the library inside the app. Deleting a song the app downloaded also removes that file from the phone. Uninstalling OmniPlayer deletes the app's library database and the songs it saved in its private storage. Songs that were already on the phone before you used OmniPlayer stay on the phone.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change.
